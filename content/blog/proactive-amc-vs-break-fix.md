Many organisations still manage IT the way they manage a car with no service plan: wait for something to break, then call someone to fix it. It feels cost-effective because you only pay when there is a problem.

The hidden cost is everything else — the hours employees cannot work, the orders that cannot be processed, the customer who walks away while the point-of-sale system restarts. For most businesses, **downtime costs far more than prevention**.

## What is an Annual Maintenance Contract?

An Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) is an agreement in which an IT partner takes responsibility for maintaining your hardware, software and networks for a fixed annual fee. There are two common types:

- **Comprehensive AMC** covers labour *and* replacement parts. It offers the most predictable costs and suits critical or ageing equipment.
- **Non-comprehensive AMC** covers labour, support and preventive maintenance, with parts billed separately. It is often more economical for newer equipment still under warranty.

Modern AMCs increasingly include remote monitoring, a service desk and cloud or SaaS support alongside traditional hardware maintenance.

## Break-fix versus proactive support

| | Break-fix | Proactive AMC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost | Unpredictable, spikes after failures | Fixed and budgeted |
| Response | Best effort, no guarantees | Contractual SLAs |
| Maintenance | Rarely performed | Scheduled preventive visits |
| Visibility | Little or no asset tracking | Full asset register and reporting |
| Incentives | Vendor earns more when things break | Partner earns more when things work |

That last row matters most. Under break-fix, your provider's revenue rises with your problems. Under a proactive contract, your partner is rewarded for keeping systems healthy.

## What good proactive support looks like

### Continuous monitoring

Servers, network devices, backups and critical applications are monitored around the clock. Many problems — a filling disk, a failing drive, an expiring certificate — are fixed before anyone notices.

### Preventive maintenance

Scheduled visits clean, update and inspect equipment, apply firmware and security patches, and test backups.

### A responsive service desk

Users raise issues by phone, email, portal or chat. Tickets are prioritised by impact, tracked against SLAs and escalated automatically when needed.

### Asset lifecycle management

Every device is recorded with its warranty, age and condition, so refresh decisions are planned rather than forced by failures.

### Transparent reporting

Monthly reports show tickets, response times, uptime, recurring issues and recommendations — so you can see exactly what the contract delivers.

> The best IT support is the kind your team barely notices, because nothing goes wrong.

## Choosing an AMC partner

When comparing providers, ask:

1. What are the **response and resolution times** for each priority level, and what happens if they are missed?
2. Is **remote monitoring** included, and what exactly is monitored?
3. How often are **preventive maintenance** visits scheduled?
4. How do you support **multiple sites** or remote employees?
5. What **reporting** will we receive each month?
6. How is the **transition** from our current provider handled?
